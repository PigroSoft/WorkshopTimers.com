Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026. Every change to this page is visible in the public history of this file.

The short version. Workshop Timer has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your settings stay in your own browser and never reach us. The only parties that can see anything are GitHub, which hosts the page, and, only if you buy and enter a Pro licence key, our payment provider Dodo Payments. We do not sell or share anyone's data.

1. Who is responsible

Jeffrey Humble, Peschkestr. 5, 12161 Berlin, Germany. Email: support@pigrosoft.com. Write to that address for any privacy question or to use your rights.

2. What stays on your device

The app saves what you set up in your browser's local storage, on your own device: your timer settings (duration, label, theme, colours, sounds, font, language), a running or paused timer so a refresh doesn't lose it, your logo and uploaded font if you add them, and your licence key if you enter one. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, except the licence key as described in section 4. You can save it as a file under Settings, and delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Legal basis: storing this on your device is strictly necessary to provide the timer you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). It is not used to identify or track you.

3. Hosting by GitHub Pages

The site is served by GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open it, your browser sends GitHub the technical data any web server receives: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent and referrer. GitHub uses this to deliver the page and keep the service secure. We do not receive or look at these logs.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in delivering a working, secure website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). This data may be processed in the USA; GitHub describes how it protects transfers from the EU, including under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework principles, in its privacy statement, which also says how long GitHub keeps logs.

4. Pro licence and Dodo Payments

Pro (the extra themes) is not on sale yet. Once it is, purchases will be handled by Dodo Payments, which acts as merchant of record: you pay Dodo, and Dodo processes your name, email address, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. Dodo states that it uses the EU's standard contractual clauses for transfers outside the EU. We see the order details Dodo shows its merchants, such as your name, email address and country, and use them only to help you with your purchase and to keep the business records the law requires (usually up to ten years under German tax and commercial law).

When you enter a licence key, your browser sends it directly to Dodo (live.dodopayments.com) together with the label "Workshop Timer · activated" and the date, so Dodo can confirm the key and count it against your device limit. Each time the app starts, it asks Dodo whether the stored key is still valid. Dodo also sees your IP address for these requests. If Dodo can't be reached, Pro keeps working. "Remove license from this device" in Settings tells Dodo to free that device slot and deletes the key from your browser.

Legal basis: carrying out your purchase (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR) and our legal record-keeping duties (Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR).

5. Voice alerts

Voice alerts use your browser's built-in speech voices. The app picks a voice that runs on your device where one exists. If your browser only offers online voices, the browser's maker (for example Google, in Chrome) may receive the fixed phrase being spoken, such as "Thirty seconds remaining", under its own terms. The phrase contains nothing about you.

6. No outside requests

All fonts, sounds and images are built into the page. The app loads nothing from Google Fonts, CDNs or other third parties. Links to GitHub, pigrosoft.com or Dodo only open when you click them.

7. What we never do

  • No cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or fingerprinting.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No email list, and no account to create.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, and to have it corrected, deleted, restricted or handed to you in a portable format (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests at any time (Art. 21 GDPR). In practice we hold nothing about you unless you buy Pro; for that, email us. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The one responsible for us is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

9. The code is open

Workshop Timer is open source, so you don't have to take this page on trust: you can read exactly what the app does in its public source code.

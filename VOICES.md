# Voice alert credits

The voice-alert clips in `voice/` were generated with [Kokoro-82M](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), an open-weight speech model released under the Apache License 2.0, using `scripts/make_voices.py`.

Kokoro's training data includes audio under Creative Commons Attribution licences, which its authors credit as follows:

- [SIWIS French Speech Synthesis Database](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353), CC BY 4.0 (French voice)
- [Koniwa](https://github.com/koniwa/koniwa) `tnc`, CC BY 3.0 (Japanese voice)
